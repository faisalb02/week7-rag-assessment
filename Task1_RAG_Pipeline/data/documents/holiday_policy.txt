Company Holiday Policy

1. The company provides 12 public holidays every year.

2. The annual holiday calendar is published by the HR department.

3. Employees are not required to work on official public holidays.

4. If employees work on a public holiday, compensatory leave may be provided.

5. Holiday calendar updates will be communicated through official email.

6. Employees should check the HR portal for the latest holiday schedule.

7. Regional holidays may vary depending on the office location.
