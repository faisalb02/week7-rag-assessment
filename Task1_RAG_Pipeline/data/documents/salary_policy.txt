Company Salary Policy

1. Employee salaries are paid on the last working day of every month.

2. Salary is credited directly to the employee's registered bank account.

3. Employees receive their salary slips through the HR portal.

4. Salary deductions may apply for unpaid leave.

5. Employees are eligible for an annual salary review.

6. Performance and company policies are considered during salary revision.

7. Salary-related questions should be directed to the HR department.
