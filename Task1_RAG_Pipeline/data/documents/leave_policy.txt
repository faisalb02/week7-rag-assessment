Company Leave Policy

1. Employees are eligible for 12 casual leaves per year.

2. Employees are eligible for 10 sick leaves per year.

3. Employees are eligible for 15 earned leaves per year.

4. Leave requests must be submitted to the reporting manager.

5. Employees should apply for planned leave at least 3 days in advance.

6. Unused casual leaves cannot be carried forward to the next year.

7. Sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
