Company Attendance Policy

1. Employees must report to work by 10:00 AM.

2. The standard working hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

3. Employees must mark their daily attendance using the company attendance system.

4. Late arrivals must be informed to the reporting manager.

5. Employees must complete their assigned working hours.

6. Attendance corrections must be requested through the HR department.

7. Repeated unauthorized absences may result in disciplinary action.
